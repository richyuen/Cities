# environment — critic review, round 1

**Score: 5.5 / 10 — FAIL** (pass needs ≥ 8.5). Functional lighting rig with real CSM shadows and a nice night dome, but every low-sun shot has a giant second "sun" with a hard horizontal seam, overcast/fog at dawn produces a near-black purple sky over a brightly lit plate, the shading light is decoupled from the sun disc, the noon sky is washed-out grey-blue, and the weather variants read as colour filters rather than weather.

Shots reviewed (every PNG opened): `shots/environment/r1c/` (12 preset shots + 17 custom + 4 full-game), `r1c-rain/`, `r1c-fog/`, `r1c-overcast/` (12 each). Reference: `docs/reference/*.jpg` (bright, warm, pastel-hazy horizons, soft shadows, glossy plastic).

## Ranked issues (most damaging first)

1. **Two suns at golden hour + hard horizontal seam.** `r1c/environment_default-18.png`, `environment_sky-18.png`, `r1c-fog/environment_default-18.png`, `r1c-overcast/environment_default-18.png`. A huge yellow glow sits ~12–15° above the horizon while the real (tiny, white) sun disc sits on the horizon. The glow is the Sky.js cloud layer: near the sun `sunColor = vSunE*Fex*…` lights the cloud sheet to a flat yellow blob, and `horizonFade = smoothstep(0, 0.03+0.06*cloudElevation, direction.y)` cuts it off with a razor-straight line (obvious at y≈290 in overcast-18, y≈450 in sky-18). With coverage 1.0 the whole sky becomes one flat glow. This is the single most visible artifact and it is present in all four variants. Fix: attenuate cloud `sunColor` by the same `skyScale`/Reinhard roll-off you apply to the sky, clamp the cloud silver/sun term, and widen the horizon fade (0.0–0.2) or fade clouds with a smooth exp; or mask the cloud pass out below ~6° and let the Preetham glow own the horizon.
2. **Overcast / fog at dawn is a near-black purple sky over a bright green plate with a crisp shadow.** `r1c-overcast/environment_sky-6h5.png`, `r1c-overcast/environment_default-6h5.png`, `r1c-fog/environment_closeup-6h5.png`. Root cause in `applyTod()`: turbidity 16–22 makes Preetham go dark at low sun, `skyScale` is then multiplied by `(1 − 0.7·twilight)` (≈0.21 at 6.5), and the twilight dome that is supposed to supply colour is suppressed by `uTwilight *= (1 − 0.75·overcast)`. Net: the one thing that should be bright (a cloud deck lit from below) goes black while sun 0.48–0.84 keeps casting hard shadows. Fix: for overcast, drive the sky from a dedicated grey/pastel cloud-deck colour ramp (keyed by elevation), not from Preetham; kill the sun (≤ 0.1) under cloud ≥ 0.8 and push hemisphere up to compensate; do not scale twilight down with overcast.
3. **Shading light is decoupled from the sun disc.** `elev = Math.max(e, 12)` in `applyTod()` pins the CSM light at ≥ 12° elevation. Consequences visible in `environment_sky-18.png` (specular streak on the plate points at the glow, 12° above the disc), and shadows do not move at all between elevation 0° and 12° while the disc does (6.5 and 18 have identical shadow lengths). Rubric: wrong sun/shadow direction −1. Fix: clamp at ~3–4° instead, and fade sun intensity with `smoothstep(-2, 6, e)` so the horizon-hugging light does not blow out; accept very long shadows — that is the golden-hour look the reference asks for.
4. **Noon sky is washed-out grey-blue, zenith not blue.** `environment_sky-12.png`, `zenith-12.png` (looking straight up: ~#8FB4D4 → #B8CCDC), `environment_default-12.png`. Reference skies are a soft but saturated blue with white cumulus. `SKY_GRAD_MIX` 0.8 toward `skyZenith (0.05,0.17,0.50)` is fighting a Preetham output that is already grey after `skyK` roll-off, and `skyTint` pushes blue but `skySat` on a near-grey signal does little. Fix: raise zenith blue (≈ 0.10, 0.30, 0.85 linear) and horizon (≈ 0.45, 0.62, 0.85), use mix 1.0 above 20° elevation, and give the day sky actual white cumulus (coverage 0.35–0.45 with `cloudDensity` ≥ 0.7 so they read as clouds, not smears).
5. **Weather reads as a colour filter, not weather.** `r1c-rain/environment_default-12.png`, `r1c-rain/environment_closeup-18.png` (rain from a cloudless golden sunset), `r1c-overcast/environment_default-12.png`, `r1c-fog/environment_default-12.png`. Under rain/overcast the ground is still saturated bright green with crisp sun shadows (sunI ≈ 0.84–0.9), the cloud deck is a pale lavender sheet with blue holes, nothing is wet or darkened, fog at intensity 0.7 leaves the 200 m plate edge fully visible (FogExp2 density 0.009 → only ~55 % at 100 m). Fix: rain/overcast → sun ≤ 0.15, hemi up, `environmentIntensity` down, darker grey deck (cloud albedo ~0.5, density 1.0), desaturate/darken ground via a global `ctx.scene` wetness hook or at least a stronger exposure drop; fog → density ≥ 0.02 at intensity 0.7 and zero sun shadows.
6. **Rain: near-camera streaks render as giant translucent slabs.** `r1c/rain-street-18.png` top-left: a 30 px-wide, 580 px-tall pale column. `rain.js` fades streaks only by far distance (`edge = 1 − smoothstep(0.32·box, 0.5·box, vDist)`); a 1.4 m streak 0.3 m from the lens fills the screen. Fix: add a near fade `smoothstep(0.5, 2.5, vDist)` and shrink `uWidth` with distance; also thin streaks are barely wind-driven at 7 m/s — use `uFall` lean ≈ 25–30° and stretch length with speed.
7. **No moon, but moonlight glare.** `r1c/sun-look-5h5.png`: a bright blue-white specular streak on the plate pointing at an empty sky; `night-look-0.png`, `environment_default-21h5.png`: no moon disc anywhere, no moon shadows (`castShadow = false`). Night has fill but no shaping. Fix: draw a moon disc in the night dome at `clock.moonDir`, and either give the moon a cheap 1024 shadow map or drop its specular (set `S.moon.intensity` lower and rely on hemi so plastics do not show a phantom highlight).
8. **Far cascade is too coarse for the city.** Cascade 3 covers 272–1600 m with a 3150 m frustum → 1.54 m texels and `normalBias` 2.0 m; cascade 2 (56–272 m) is 0.25 m texels / 0.34 m bias. At the game's `overview` camera (≈ 600–900 m) every building shadow lands in cascade 3 and will be a blob offset 2 m; 1 m Blox elements will lose their shadows entirely. Not yet visible because the full game has no buildings (`fullgame-18.png` is flat terrain), but it will be. Fix: 4 cascades or `maxFar` driven by camera height (e.g. `min(1600, 3 × camera.y + 200)`), practical split λ≈0.6, and cap `normalBias` at ~0.3 m.
9. **Glossy plastic shows no sky reflection.** `r1c/close3m-12.png`, `environment_closeup-12.png`: brick faces are matte two-tone, the glass brick is frosted milk with no fresnel or sky reflection. `ENV_INTENSITY` 0.35 on a PMREM whose sky is already scaled by 0.32 gives an IBL ~0.1× the sun. Materials are core's, but IBL strength is environment's. Fix: `environmentIntensity` ≈ 0.8–1.0 relative to a properly exposed sky; verify with a chrome sphere in the showcase.
10. **Showcase floats in a void.** `r1c/far300m-12.png`, `far300m-18.png`: a 200 m plate hovering in uniform grey-blue/brown; below-horizon sky is a smear. A lighting showcase needs a horizon to judge fog/aerial perspective against. Also `environment:closeup` and `environment:sky` presets have a yellow/red tower sliver clipping the right edge (`environment_closeup-12.png`, `environment_sky-*.png`). Fix: add a 2 km ground disc (dark green, no studs) under the plate and move the sky preset 3 m left.
11. **Fog colour ignores clouds.** `computeFogColor()` mirrors the Preetham shader but not the cloud composite, so under overcast the fog tint is clear-sky Preetham at turbidity 16 (brownish at low sun, `r1c/fullgame-18.png` reads as a brown mud filter over the whole map). Fix: blend fog colour toward the cloud-deck colour by `wf.cloud`.
12. **Code nits.** (a) `sweep()` traverses the whole scene every 120 frames and on every spawn event — fine now, O(N) later with thousands of buildings; batch via the events' payload materials instead. (b) `dispose()` does not dispose `S.sky.geometry`, and `S.envHours/camKey/sweepAt` are not reset (init re-sets them, so harmless today). (c) PMREM regen every 1.25 s at timeScale 1 makes reflections step; blend two env maps or regen every 0.25 h. (d) `csmPatch.js` is well guarded: marker + symbol checks, `installed` memo, try/catch, and a real single-light fallback (4096 map, 840 m box snapped to texels). The fallback has never been exercised in a screenshot — worth one run with the markers deliberately broken.

## What already works
- CSM shadows are clean: no acne, no peter-panning, no visible cascade seams at 3 m (`close3m-12.png` stud shadows crisp) or along a grazing 200 m view (`seam-low-16.png`); tower shadow still present at 300 m (`far300m-18.png`).
- Night is genuinely night: deep-blue dome, Milky Way band and twinkling stars (`environment_sky-21h5.png`, `sun-look-5h5.png` pre-dawn is the best sky in the set), fog at night matches the sky (`r1c-fog/environment_default-21h5.png`).
- Dawn gradient on the clear closeup (`environment_closeup-6h5.png`) — blue to pink with long shadows — is close to the target look, and the full game at 07:00 (`fullgame-skyline-7.png`) gets proper aerial perspective on the hills.
- Zero console errors in 70 captures, deterministic (seeded rng), single-digit env draw calls, tidy API surface.
