# environment — critic review, round 2

**Score: 7.5 / 10 — FAIL** (pass needs ≥ 8.5). This is a near-total rewrite (three/addons `Sky.js` removed, replaced by a
custom analytic sky in `sky.js` + `skyMath.js`) and it shows: 10 of the 12 round-1 issues are fixed or materially
improved — the two-suns/hard-seam bug is gone, overcast/fog dawn no longer goes near-black, the sun/shadow direction
is coupled at 2.5° instead of 12°, noon is a real saturated blue with cumulus, weather now reads as weather, rain
streaks are natural, a real moon disc exists, and glossy plastic finally reflects the sky. The one significant
regression is the showcase's new ground disc + hill ring (added to fix "floats in a void"): at every distance beyond
a few hundred metres the flat ground disc fully saturates into fog colour while the raised hills don't, producing a
hard, uniformly-coloured "sea line" across the whole horizon in nearly every showcase screenshot. It reads as a
rendering bug even though it is a physically-consequent result of a flat 2.6 km disc + FogExp2. The full game (real
terrain, no flat disc) does **not** show this artifact, so it is confined to `showcase.js`, but since the showcase is
the primary way this module is judged, it keeps the score out of AAA-with-nits territory.

Shots reviewed (every PNG opened): `shots/environment/r2c/` (12 preset shots), `r2c-rain/`, `r2c-fog/`,
`r2c-overcast/` (12 each), `r2c-custom/` (13: 3m/300m/600m shadows, grazing-seam, glass reflection ×2, moon
clear/cloudy, zenith), `r2c-fullgame/` (overview × 7/12/18/21.5). Builder's own round-2 shots in `r2-dev*/` were not
overwritten. Reference: `docs/REFERENCE.md` (no `docs/reference/` images present).

## Verification of the 12 round-1 issues
1. **Two suns / hard horizon seam** — **FIXED.** All golden-hour shots (`r2c/environment_default-18.png`, `environment_sky-18.png`, and the `-18` shot in every weather variant) show one small sun disc with a smooth Henyey-Greenstein halo and no second glow blob, no razor-edge horizon cut.
2. **Overcast/fog dawn near-black sky over bright ground** — **FIXED.** `r2c-overcast/environment_default-6h5.png` is a soft pale-grey overcast sky, ground is evenly and softly lit, no crisp sun shadow visible under the deck.
3. **Shading light decoupled from sun disc (clamped ≥12°)** — **FIXED** (floor lowered to `MIN_LIGHT_ELEV = 2.5°`, `index.js:20`). Golden-hour shading direction now visually tracks the disc; long low-angle shadows are present and specular highlights track the visible sun.
4. **Noon sky washed-out grey-blue** — **FIXED.** `r2c/environment_default-12.png`, `environment_sky-12.png`, `r2c-custom/zenith-12.png` show a properly saturated blue zenith fading to a lighter blue horizon with distinct white cumulus, not the previous grey wash.
5. **Weather reads as a colour filter** — **FIXED** (mostly). Overcast, fog and rain each now have a distinct visual identity: overcast is a soft uniform greyscale deck (`r2c-overcast/*`), fog is a heavy near-white/grey wash that genuinely obscures the far plate (`r2c-fog/environment_default-12.png`), rain is a dim green-grey storm light with visible streaks. None of the three show the old "saturated green plate + crisp sun shadow under an opaque deck" combination.
6. **Giant rain slabs** — **FIXED.** `r2c-rain/environment_closeup-18.png` and `environment_default-*.png` show thin, wind-leaned streaks at plausible density and width; no full-screen slabs near the camera.
7. **No moon / unexplained glare** — **FIXED**, with a caveat. `r2c-custom/moon-clear-3h5.png` shows a proper moon disc with a maria texture and soft glow. Under `weather=cloudy:0.4` (`moon-clouds-3h5.png`) the moon is completely invisible rather than dimmed/veiled — coverage at that intensity computes to ~0.87 (`skyMath.js` `coverage = lerp(0.46,1.0,smoothstep(0,0.9,cloud))`), and since the cloud mix happens *after* the moon draw in `sky.js`'s fragment shader, a cloud patch fully overpaints the moon whenever the sampled ray falls in a covered region — likely, but not sampled at this coverage. Real skies still show a bright veiled glow through thin cloud; here the transition is binary (visible/gone) rather than a dimming.
8. **Coarse far cascade** — **IMPROVED, not fully re-verified.** `updateCascades()` now grows `maxFar` and the cascade splits with the camera-to-target distance and pitch, versus r1's fixed 1600 m/3-break split, which was the actual complaint (city-scale shadows landing in a fixed, too-coarse cascade 3). No acne, seams, or detached-shadow blobs were visible in `r2c-custom/shadow-3m-*.png` (crisp, tight contact shadows) or the `shadow-300m-*`/`shadow-600m-tight-golden.png` shots, but the showcase scene's few small towers don't fill enough of the frame at 300–600 m to conclusively stress-test cascade-3 texel density the way a dense downtown at the game's real `overview` preset would. Recommend the integrator re-check this specifically once `buildings` has real geometry.
9. **No sky reflection on plastic** — **FIXED.** `r2c-custom/glass-closeup-noon.png` and `-golden.png` show a clear soft reflection band of the sky/horizon on the studded baseplate and a visible fresnel sheen on the glass brick — a real improvement (`ENV_INTENSITY` raised to 0.9, `index.js:17`). Minor: the reflection reads as a somewhat sharp-edged pale "patch" rather than a smooth photographic gradient (see ranked issues).
10. **Showcase floats in a void** — **PARTIALLY FIXED, new regression.** `showcase.js` now adds a 2.6 km ground disc + a ring of 26 procedural hills, giving the plate a horizon to sit against (`r2c/environment_default-*.png`). But at any distance the flat disc's FogExp2 optical path saturates to solid fog colour while the raised hills (shorter grazing path) don't, producing a hard, perfectly uniform-coloured band that reads as a sea horizon under every plate/hill line in nearly every default/sky shot, and gets worse the farther the camera sits (`r2c-custom/shadow-300m-golden.png`, `shadow-600m-tight-golden.png` — the plate looks like a tiny island in a flat ocean). This is now the single most visible defect in the module. See ranked issue #1.
11. **Fog colour ignores clouds** — **FIXED.** `computeFogColor()` now samples the same analytic model (`skySample()`, which folds in `cloudAmb`/`cloudLit` as a coverage-weighted deck) instead of a clear-sky-only Preetham mirror. Fog colour in `r2c-fog/*` and `r2c-overcast/*` matches the grey deck, and `r2c-fullgame` shots show no brown-mud cast.
12. **Code nits** — (a) sweep() unchanged, still O(N) traversal — acceptable per r1 note, not urgent. (b) **dispose() now disposes `S.sky.mesh.geometry`/`material`** and resets `envHours/camKey/cascadeKey/sweepAt` (`index.js:397-414`) — fixed. (c) PMREM regen cadence is unchanged (`ENV_REGEN_MIN_MS = 250`, `dh > step` gating) — reflections still step roughly every 0.02–0.08 h of sun movement; not re-verified as a visible issue this round but not addressed either. (d) `csmPatch.js` unchanged, still well-guarded; the single-light fallback path was still not exercised in any screenshot.

## Ranked issues (most damaging first)
1. **"Sea horizon" seam from the showcase's ground disc + hills.** `r2c/environment_default-6h5.png`, `-12.png`, `-18.png`, `-21h5.png`, `environment_sky-6h5/12/21h5.png`, and every weather variant's `default-*` shot, worst in `r2c-custom/shadow-300m-golden.png` and `shadow-600m-tight-golden.png`. The flat 2.6 km ground disc fogs out to a perfectly uniform, fairly saturated colour band (teal-green by day, sepia at golden hour, near-black at night) with a razor-sharp edge where the raised hill ring begins, because the hills' shorter/steeper view path through FogExp2 fogs less than the disc's near-grazing path. It reads exactly like a flat sea meeting a coastline, which is distracting and looks like a bug even though it is a physically-consequent fog artifact. Fix: either give the ground disc some height variation near its outer radius (so it fogs at the same rate as the hills), reduce the disc radius so hills start closer in and the transition is less visible, or blend a subtle gradient/dither into the far ground rather than a flat plastic colour so the fog transition doesn't read as a hard material change.
2. **Far-cascade quality at real city-overview distances is still not conclusively verified.** The adaptive-cascade logic (`updateCascades()`) is a real, well-reasoned improvement over r1's fixed 1600 m split, and nothing broken was observed in any shot taken this round, but the showcase's small building cluster couldn't be framed to fill enough of a 300–600 m shot to see cascade-3 texel density the way the game's dense downtown will. Not a fail on its own since no evidence of the r1 defect was found, but flag for a follow-up check once `buildings` has real geometry at the actual `overview` preset.
3. **Moon fully disappears (rather than dims/glows) under moderate cloud.** `r2c-custom/moon-clouds-3h5.png` (weather `cloudy:0.4`, coverage computes to ~0.87) shows no moon or moon-glow at all versus a clean moon disc in `moon-clear-3h5.png`. Because the cloud composite is mixed in after the moon term in `sky.js`'s fragment shader, any cloud pixel fully overwrites the moon rather than letting a veiled glow show through. Fix: add a moon-glow contribution to the cloud "lit" term (similar to the existing sun-glow `silver` rim-light term) so a moon behind thin cloud still shows a soft glow, and/or don't zero the moon at the coverage levels 'cloudy' weather typically produces.
4. **Sky/ground reflection reads as a soft rectangular "patch" rather than a smooth gradient.** `r2c-custom/glass-closeup-noon.png`, `glass-closeup-golden.png`: the reflected sky brightness on the studded baseplate has a visible near/far boundary rather than a continuous Fresnel falloff, likely from the low-res (768×1024, `regenEnv()`) PMREM combined with the studs' geometry. Minor relative to r1 (which had none at all), but worth a look — increasing PMREM resolution slightly or smoothing the reflection falloff term would help sell it as a real environment reflection rather than a baked patch.
5. **PMREM regen cadence unaddressed (r1 nit c).** Reflections still step roughly every 1.25 s at `timeScale 1`/noon (`ENV_REGEN_MIN_MS=250`, gated by `dh > step` where `step=0.02–0.08` h) — not verified as visible this round, but the underlying cause from r1 is unchanged; low priority.
6. **Hills read as flat, almost featureless silhouettes at low sun.** `r2c/environment_sky-6h5.png`, `-12.png`, `-21h5.png`: the hill ring (`M.plastic('darkGreen', {roughness:0.8})`, no cast/receive shadow) shows almost no internal shading gradient — each hill is close to a single flat tone. Combined with issue #1, this makes the horizon read as cut-out shapes rather than terrain. Low priority (showcase-only), but giving hills `receiveShadow`/a slight AO or multi-tone gradient would help sell depth.

## What already works
- Golden hour is now genuinely golden: one sun disc, warm pastel purple-to-orange gradient with soft cumulus, long correctly-directed shadows, and a real specular glint on the ground pointing at the disc (`r2c/environment_default-18.png`, `environment_sky-18.png`).
- Noon reads as a crisp, saturated blue sky with white clouds (`r2c/environment_default-12.png`, `zenith-12.png`); night is deep blue with a convincing star field, Milky Way band, and a textured moon disc with maria detail (`environment_closeup-21h5.png`, `moon-clear-3h5.png`).
- Weather is now legible at a glance: overcast/fog/rain each produce a visually distinct, desaturated, shadow-less mood instead of a tinted clear-sky filter, and fog genuinely obscures distance (`r2c-fog/environment_default-12.png`).
- Glossy plastic and the glass brick now show real sky reflections and fresnel sheen; CSM shadows remain clean at 3 m (no acne/peter-panping, `shadow-3m-noon.png`, `shadow-3m-golden.png`); zero console errors and Math.random-free across ~46 captures this round.
