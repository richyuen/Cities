## Ranked issues (most damaging first)

1. **Camera-interior hollow-building bug (round-1 #2) is genuinely mitigated but not fixed, and this round's own testing sharpens exactly where the remaining gap is.** The `interiorLiner()` mitigation (per-shell inward-facing duplicate, `generator.js` line 51) works as designed: my independent test on the residential tower — a different building than the builder's own `r2-custom/interior-*.png` shots, which all used the office tower — shows a fully solid interior with no sky/terrain leaks at a camera distance that respects `core/camera.js`'s `OrbitControls.minDistance=4`. But that same constraint is itself informative: `apply()` sets `camera.position`/`controls.target` directly, then calls `controls.update()`, which recomputes `camera.position` from `target + sphericalOffset` under `minDistance=4` and `maxPolarAngle=0.495π` — so *any* request for a genuinely close-up or steeply-angled interior view silently gets pushed back out by the shared camera rig before it ever reaches buildings' own 0.14m liner margin. In other words: the liner's real coverage is currently untestable at the exact close/shallow angles the builder's own `docs/core-requests/buildings.md` flags as still-failing ("very close to a corner/window gap at a shallow angle," "the vertical gap right at the roofline") — because the *tooling* itself can't easily reach those angles either, via the normal `setCamera` path. That's not proof the remaining gaps are fine; it's the opposite: the only viewpoints from which this module has ever been shown to leak sky are viewpoints the shared `OrbitControls` config makes awkward to reach on purpose (`minDistance`/`maxPolarAngle` exist to keep the camera sane), which means an unusual camera path (e.g. a future free-fly debug camera, or controls tuned differently) could still expose the hole. This is correctly filed as a core-camera request rather than something buildings can fully own — that's the right call, not a dodge — but the module's visual identity still has an unresolved geometry-robustness edge, so it keeps the round below the AAA bar. **Fix (still, per the filed core-request):** XZ collision/AABB-reject in `core/camera.js`, or accept the mitigation as final and document the residual gap as a known limitation rather than an open item.

2. *(new, very minor, cosmetic)* **A small dark seam artifact appears where the office tower's now-inflated corner pilaster meets a per-floor spandrel band, visible only at sub-1.5m custom-camera range.** `shots/buildings/r2c-custom/office-pilaster-extreme.png` (camera ~1.3m from the corner, computed from the exact generator formula, i.e. closer than `OrbitControls.minDistance=4` would ever allow via normal orbit) shows a hairline dark sliver right where the pilaster's `bevelBox` rounded edge (`generator.js` line 308, `inflate=0.07`) meets the spandrel band `plainBox` (line 317, sized to `halfW*2+inflate*2`) at the same corner. Likely the spandrel band's flat edge doesn't fully tuck behind the pilaster's rounded bevel at that exact corner pixel. Not reachable through the normal camera rig (see issue 1's discussion of `minDistance=4`), so very low real-world impact — flagging for completeness since the brief asked for an extreme pilaster closeup specifically. **Fix:** extend the spandrel band the same `inflate` amount at the corners only, or trim it to stop just short of the pilaster's footprint instead of running through it.

3. *(informational, not a round-2 regression)* **Actual triangle cost per building at scale (~2,900–3,240 tris) runs roughly double the module's own documented target ("~1000-1500 tris/building average," `generator.js` line 12).** Confirmed unchanged in character from round 1 (round 1 marginal: 2,876 tris/bldg; round 2 marginal: 2,909 tris/bldg — the new liner/spandrel geometry adds only ~1%, see perf section above). At today's showcase scale this is a non-issue (233 buildings → 755k tris, comfortably inside any real budget), but extrapolated to a genuinely full 1500-building city per the module's own comment, buildings alone would be ~4.4–4.9M tris — above the "3M full-city cap" the same comment references. Not counted against this round's score (it's a pre-existing condition neither introduced nor worsened here), but worth a follow-up validation once a real full-city scene exists, since it wasn't caught as a numeric mismatch in the round-1 review either.
