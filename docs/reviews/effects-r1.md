## Ranked issues

1. **The effects showcase never loads `environment`, so every critic PNG is lit by `core/fallbackLights`** (`deps: []` in `src/effects/index.js`). Result across all 32 batch shots: no sun shadows, flat single-colour sky, and a constant bright PMREM env map so **"night" (21.5) is as bright as an overcast afternoon** (`r1/effects_default-21h5.png`, `r1/effects_tower-21h5.png` - green baseplate fully lit). 6.5 / 18 / 21.5 are near-identical because the fallback sun is at intensity 0 for all three. Neither bloom nor night-lift can be judged from this. With `?only=effects,environment` (`r1-extra/env-tower-night-on.png`, `env-default-golden-on.png`) the module actually looks good - warm window glow, stars, long shadows. Fix: `deps: ['environment']` (or at minimum have `showcase()` require it) so the module is shown on the pipeline it will ship on.
2. **AO is barely there.** `setDebug('ao')` at closeup and 3 m macro (`r1-extra/closeup-noon-aodebug.png`, `macro-3m-aodebug.png`) shows ~5-15 % darkening only inside tight inside corners; open baseplate 220 vs sky 229 in the tone-mapped debug view. Under 0.96 m bricks sitting on the plate there is a thin line at best, studs get nothing. Low vs high quality at closeup (`closeup-noon-q-low.png` vs `-q-high.png`) is hard to tell apart - the 2 ms is buying almost nothing. Cause: `radius 2.2 m` with `distanceFallOff 0.6` and `thickness 1.2` spread the term so wide it averages out; `AO_INTENSITY 0.9` then multiplies a near-1 value. Suggest radius ~0.6-0.9 m, thickness ~0.4, scale ~2-3, and apply AO with a contrast curve (`pow(ao, 2)`) so brick creases and stud bases read; shrink the denoiser radius (4 px -> 2-3) to stop the smear seen as a dark wedge on the blue brick face in `macro-3m-aodebug.png` (x 1050-1200, y 780-1000) - a silhouette halo from depth-reconstructed normals.
3. **Sky is rendered differently with post on vs off when the real `environment` is loaded.** three's `Sky` shader has no `tonemapping_fragment`/`colorspace_fragment`, so in the direct path it is written raw (golden-hour left sky clips to 255/255/255, `env-default-golden-off.png`), while inside the composer `OutputPass` tone-maps + sRGB-encodes it (227/203/179; top-centre 136 vs 183). The `skyBypass` in `grade.js` only covers `scene.background.isColor`, not the sky mesh. Whichever is the intended look, the two paths must agree - environment tuned its sky on the direct path. Needs an integrator decision: either effects extends the bypass to far-plane pixels regardless of background type, or environment sets its sky in linear HDR and relies on the composer.
4. **Bloom is too heavy on the (fallback-lit) tower and lamps bloom in full daylight.** In `r1/effects_tower-21h5.png` the whole facade is haloed cream and the sky above the tower is lifted +30 levels (top-centre 103/119/126 vs 74/101/121 post off). At noon the lamp bricks still carry a visible halo (`r1/effects_tower-12.png`, `sky-horizon-noon-on.png`) because `emissive(...,3)` clears the 1.5 day threshold and the showcase never switches emissives off by time. Suggest radius 0.4 -> 0.25, night strength 0.5 -> 0.35, and gate showcase emissives on `clock.isNight` (as buildings/props are required to). With real environment (`env-tower-night-on.png`) the glow is close to right - so most of this is scene brightness, not the pass, but the transYellow windows also tone-map to pale cream rather than warm yellow.
5. **Showcase geometry bugs visible in every closeup**: the `mediumStoneGrey` 2x2 at layer 2 (`ox-0.4, oz-0.4`) interpenetrates the rotated `brightOrange` 2x4 (`ox+1.6, oz-0.4`, spans x in [ox, ox+3.2]) by half a stud -> the diagonal grey/orange seam at (~930, 420) in `r1/effects_closeup-12.png`, present with post off too. The tower's plate bands at `0.32 + i*5.76` slice through window rows 2 and 4 (`r1/effects_tower-12.png`, y ~200 and ~480) - frames poke through the band. Windows are lit at noon.
6. **`effects:night` preset and `night` variant are dead weight**: the preset is the same camera as `effects:default`, and the variant only calls `clock.set(21.5)` which `--tod` immediately overrides - the r1-night batch shows the same content as r1. Give night its own camera (low, close to the tower/lamps) and stage emissives.
7. Minor code notes: `api.setBloom({threshold})` is silently undone on the next `time:changed` (the comment admits it) - store an override; `applyQuality()` compares/mutates `sceneRT.samples` and disposes the target, and it is called on every resize; `S.rng` is forked twice (init and showcase) - harmless, pick one; identical staged frames produce different hashes across runs (`r1/effects_default-18` vs `r1/effects_night-18`), suggesting a per-frame noise/time dependency worth confirming for deterministic screenshots.

## What already works
- Anti-aliasing is genuinely clean: MSAA x4 into the HDR target + SMAA after `OutputPass` gives crisp stud rims and brick bevels at 3 m and at overview (`macro-3m-on.png`, `far-overview-on.png`); the post-off comparison shows obvious jaggies without it.
- Pipeline plumbing is correct: read buffer pinned to the MSAA target so GTAO reads the live depth, no double tone mapping on the Color-background path, resize and dispose behave, no console errors in any of 61 JSONs, quality tiers switch live.
- With the real environment loaded, night bloom on emissive windows and lamps reads as a Lego city at night should - warm, soft, not smeared (`env-tower-night-on.png`).
